# Borrow from another domain

**Works for:** code, architecture, design, writing

**The move:** Find a field that already solved the same shape of problem and steal its pattern.

**Ask:**
- What is the abstract shape of this problem (queueing, matching, routing, caching, negotiation)?
- Which unrelated field lives with that shape every day (logistics, kitchens, air traffic, libraries)?
- What is their standard trick?

**Example:** Retry storms after an outage → borrow "metering lights" from highway on-ramps: token-bucket admission.

**Watch out:** Analogies are persuasive. The measurement decides, not the elegance of the metaphor.
